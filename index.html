A 1 MWe solar thermal (CSP) plant uses mirrors to concentrate sunlight and produce steam to drive a turbine. Common technologies are parabolic troughs, central towers, or linear Fresnel. For 1 MWe net output, about 4.4 MWₜₕ is needed at the turbine inlet. With ~45% solar field efficiency and 800 W/m² DNI, aperture area is ~12,000 m²; with storage, 17–20 thousand m².
